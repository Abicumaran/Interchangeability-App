PROXIMA DATASET COMPATIBILITY

Primary expected replicate-level structure:
- batch_id
- bloodSampleId ending in donor-specimen-replicate, for example D05-Mc-2
- global_flag
- MHS analyte columns
- matched Sysmex columns ending in _ref
- optional analyte flag columns ending in _flag

The app also allows explicit donor, specimen-type and replicate columns and can
synthesize the canonical bloodSampleId internally.

Supported default analytes and MHS columns:
RBC=RBC; WBC=WBC_2; NEUT=NEUT_2; LYMPH=LYMPH_2; MXD=MXD_2;
PLT=PLT; MCV=MCV; RDW=RDW; MCH=MCH; MCHC=MCHC; HGB=HGB; HCT=HCT.

All MHS/reference/flag columns are remappable in the app. Specimen labels are
read from the uploaded file and may include Mc, Mc2, MK2, MK3, Dir, Tas, or
other study labels.
